Launcher updates and player setup

- The launcher automatically installs newer published launcher and mod releases while the game is closed.
- First setup installs the bundled starter save for your Steam account when no Seamless Co-op save exists.
- Change your character's in-game name from Settings > Player. Name edits keep a backup.
- Existing saves and private room codes are preserved during updates.
- Use the all-unlocked save anytime from Settings, with confirmation and a backup first.
- Steam account detection refreshes automatically whenever you press Play.
- Import your local Steam save with all its original names and progress from Settings > Player. The vanilla save is kept unchanged and the existing mod save is backed up.
- Custom main-menu background, stretched across the full game title screen.
- Private updates use a read-only GitHub token stored encrypted for your Windows user; source and releases share this repository.

Everyone in a match needs the same mod version. This player build supports up to 6 players and starts with at least 2 after the fill wait.
