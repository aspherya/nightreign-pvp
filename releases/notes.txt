Arena fixes and a version check

- An Arena round now ends when a player's HP drops to 1. Nobody goes down any more, so the loser no longer dies at the start of the next round and no level is lost.
- Arena rounds have a clock: a "One minute left!" message, then at the 3-minute limit an overtime of up to 60 seconds where both players slowly lose HP; the first to reach 1 HP loses the round.
- The launcher no longer starts an old version of the mod. Update through the launcher app (it does it by itself) or download the new zip.

Everyone in a match needs the same mod version. This player build supports up to 6 players and starts with at least 2 after the fill wait.
