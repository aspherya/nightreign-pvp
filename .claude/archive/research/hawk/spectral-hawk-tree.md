# Spectral Hawk Tree

Source: [Fextralife Elden Ring Nightreign Wiki](https://eldenringnightreign.wiki.fextralife.com/Spectral_Hawk_Tree)

## Overview

Spectral Hawk Trees are glowing blue trees in Limveld that serve as launch points for Spectral Hawks. Interacting with one summons a hawk that carries the player along a fixed route across the land. The route is shown on the map and can help players travel quickly toward safe areas.

Players can steer away from the designated route; doing so makes the hawk descend. Otherwise, the hawk follows the full route if the player does not move.

## Locations

- The locations are fixed and do not change between expeditions.
- The trees are revealed on the map at the start of an expedition.

## In-game description

> Trees where spectral hawks rest their wings. Warriors can take to the skies via the spectral road. You can find these roads marked on the map.
