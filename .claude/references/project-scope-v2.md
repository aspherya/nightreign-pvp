Elden Ring Nightreign — Battle Royale PvP Mod
Core concept: Multiple teams compete in a two-night expedition. Players explore Limveld, gather equipment, defeat PvE enemies, and fight rival teams. Death causes players to drop their entire inventory, but they can respawn until the final arena closes on Night 2.
The last surviving team wins and chooses to escape with its loot or challenge the Nightlord for greater rewards.

Night 1 — Survival (no boss)
The Night's Tide shrinks the playable area.
No nighttime boss encounter.
Players can continue fighting each other and PvE enemies.
Respawning remains enabled.
Surviving teams progress to Day 2.

Day 2 — Final preparation
The map expands again.
Teams can upgrade their loadouts and farm runes.
Strong PvE enemies guard valuable equipment.
PvP deaths continue to cause complete inventory loss and respawning.
Players prepare for the mandatory final battle.

Night 2 — Battle Royale finale
All teams enter the same PvP arena.
The arena progressively shrinks.
Respawns continue until the arena finishes closing.
Once fully closed, respawns are permanently disabled.
The last team standing wins.

4. Night 2 — Final arena rules
The second night has two stages:
Stage 1 — Arena contraction
All teams fight while the arena shrinks. Death still causes a full inventory drop and respawn with starting items. Teams can recover dropped equipment and continue competing.
Stage 2 — Sudden death
Once the arena reaches its final size, respawns are disabled for everyone. From this point onward, deaths are permanent. The battle continues until only one team has living players.

Additional arena rules:
No new PvE bosses spawn inside the final PvP arena.
Existing character abilities and team revives remain usable.
Players who respawn during contraction re-enter at a safe position inside the current playable area.
If combat stalls during sudden death, escalating environmental damage forces an eventual winner.
A team wins as soon as every opposing team has been eliminated after respawns are disabled.
5. Winner's decision
After winning Night 2, the surviving team gets a choice.

Escape with loot
Safely extract with all acquired loot and the Battle Royale victory rewards.
Guaranteed reward, no further combat.

Challenge the Nightlord
Face the Nightlord with the equipment collected during the match.
Defeating it grants greater rewards; failure forfeits most of the loot.
The team that wins PvP is officially the Battle Royale champion regardless of whether it subsequently defeats the Nightlord.
6. Additional balancing rules
No kill farming rewards: Kills provide dropped inventory, not extra levels or free high-tier loot.
No permanent equipment: Every match starts with the Nightfarer's default gear.
Respawn anti-camping: Respawns are spread across safe areas of the current zone.
Dropped items remain lootable: Gear stays on the ground for a reasonable duration, allowing the victim's squad to recover it.
No formal alliances: All rival squads remain hostile, even if they temporarily cooperate against PvE threats.
Final arena supply: Limited neutral loot remains accessible, allowing players who respawn shortly before sudden death to rebuild a basic loadout.

The central distinction from a conventional Battle Royale is that death before the final closure is a loot setback, not an elimination. Teams can lose everything, recover through exploration or PvP, and still fight for the win. Only the closing moments of Night 2 determine the ultimate survivor.
